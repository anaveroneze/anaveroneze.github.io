## Ana Veroneze Solórzano

<img class="profile-picture" src="img/ana.png" alt="Ana Veroneze Solorzano">

Researcher in High-Performance Computing, located in Boston, USA. Computer Scientist with a MSc from [UFRGS](http://www.ufrgs.br/ufrgs/inicial) and a BSc from [UFSM](http://ufsm.br). Currently, PhD candidate in Computer Engineering at Northeastern University in Boston, in the [Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering](https://ece.northeastern.edu), advised by Prof. [Devesh Tiwari](https://coe.northeastern.edu/people/tiwari-devesh/) - **expected graduation in Fall 2027**.  

My biggest motivation is on working with large-scale systems leaders, policy-makers, and users, to develop tools to make these systems ready for current and upcoming demands.

Email: solorzano.an@northeastern.edu
